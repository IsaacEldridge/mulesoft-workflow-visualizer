# Trailhead Content Quality Rubric

This rubric is used to evaluate badge and trail content quality. The rubric applies to both badge units and trail structures.

## Badge Content Quality Rubric

| Rule | Excellent (3 points) | Good / Needs Refinement (2 points) | Not Approved / Needs Significant Improvement (1 point) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Template** | The trail uses the current template, which contains the formatting and metadata configuration to ensure a smooth publishing process. [Public: Enhanced Trail Proposal](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IHi2XU4Qr16Dugn1t3PihjsdYxqfIcDMVsRQdLoJ-Bo/edit?usp=sharing) for public trails | N/A | The trail does not use the current template. |
| **Learner objective** | A learner objective is selected and it aligns with the content. | A learner objective is selected and it aligns with the content for the most part. Some content may not align. | No learner objective is selected. Or the learner objective does not align well with the content. |
| **Role** | Majority (90%+) of steps focuses on a single role. | There are several steps in the Trail that have different roles, but the intended audience still has focus and the content meant for other roles helps supplement the learning. | Multiple roles are represented in the trail making it difficult to identify the audience. |
| **Level** | Majority (90%+) are at the same learning level. | There are several steps in the Trail that vary in level, but the learning path is logical—they progress from Foundational to Intermediate, Intermediate to Advanced. | Multiple steps vary in level, the learning path is illogical and creates an obstacle for learners to follow along. |
| **Links** | All non-badge steps have URLs. | N/A | Non-badge steps are missing URLs. |
| **Content length** | Content meets Trailhead standards for content length. 1-7 milestones, 2-7 steps per milestone | N/A | Content length does not meet Trailhead standards. |
| **Names and descriptions** | Content meets Trailhead standards for name and description structures. | N/A | Content does not meet Trailhead standards for names and description structure. |
| **Appropriate trail content** | All content types are approved. | N/A | Non-approved content types are used. |
| **Grammar** | The content is grammatically correct. | There are instances where grammar can be improved, but the ratio of correct grammar is above 90%. | The grammar in this content needs significant improvement. |
| **Brand Alignment** | The content is aligned with approved terms, existing published content on Trailhead, Salesforce Help, Salesforce Release Notes, and so on. | There are minor discrepancies between the content and approved terms, existing published content on Trailhead, Salesforce Help, Salesforce Release Notes, and so on. | There are significant discrepancies between the content and existing published content on Trailhead, Salesforce Help, Salesforce Release Notes, and so on. |

## Publishability Score Scale

| Total Score | Trail Quality Rating | Action Required |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **25-30** | ⭐ Excellent | The Trail meets overall quality standards and may be published after final stakeholder review and feedback resolution. |
| **21-24** | ✅ Good | The Trail is solid but need refinements to meet overall quality standards. |
| **11-20** | ⚠️ Needs Improvement | The Trail requires moderate to deep updates to meet quality standards. |
| **10** | ❌ Not Approved / Needs Significant Improvement | The Trail requires significant revision to meet quality standards. |

## Scoring Guidelines

- **Total possible score**: 42 points (14 rubric areas × 3 points each)
- **Publishability Percentage**: (Total Score / 42) × 100
- **Each rubric area must be scored**; if an area is missing from content, score it as "Not Approved / Needs Significant Improvement" (1 point)
- Do not modify the rubric, score, or percentage calculations—accuracy is critical for analytics

## Review Process

When reviewing content:
1. Parse the source material thoroughly
2. Check every line and section against guidelines and standards
3. Capture all formatting (headings, lists, images with alt text)
4. Apply the rubric to calculate a Total Score
5. Verify all features, product names, and functionality against official Salesforce sources
6. Identify deprecated features or outdated terminology
7. Cross-reference content against Salesforce release notes and help documentation

## Feedback Format

When providing feedback on content:
1. Give a critical analysis highlighting strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement
2. Provide a table with: rubric area, score, and brief summary of issues
3. State the Badge Quality Rating and Action Required
4. Include the Publishability Percentage for reporting
5. Include the Publishability Score
6. Recommend up to 10 actionable improvements aligned with Trailhead standards
7. Frame recommendations consistently for easy implementation
8. Guide reviewers by mentioning specific landmarks (unit number, heading, paragraph text, bullet number)
